Job Scan Console

Your Job Search, Step by Step

You talk to Claude. It scans, checks, and preps — and it never sends anything or makes claims your résumé can't back.

made for AI Build Club workshop students

The happy path

Fourteen steps, four stages. Just follow them in order the first time through.

SETUP SCAN APPLY INTERVIEW
  1. 1
    Setup
    You do Ask Claude: Onboard me (have your résumé PDF nearby)

    This starts a guided conversation — Claude reads your résumé and asks you questions, so you don't have to know what to type next.

    You get resume.md your résumé as text
  2. 2
    Setup
    You do Confirm your career facts and answer a few questions
    You get facts.md every claim with its source — this powers the honesty checks
  3. 3
    Setup
    You do Answer the preferences interview (titles, location, salary floor)
    You get profile.md what every scan searches for
  4. 4
    Setup
    You do Start the console: python3 -m http.server 8642 --bind 127.0.0.1

    This turns your own laptop into a tiny, private web server — nothing leaves your machine — so the console page can load its files.

    You do Then open localhost:8642/console/ in your browser

    That's the address for the server you just started — think of it as a local-only web address only you can see.

    You get console your live dashboard
  5. 5
    Scan
    You do /scan-jobs

    Type this in Claude to have it search for open roles that match your profile.

    You get reports/ a ranked report: fit score out of 10 with reasons, real apply links
  6. 6
    Scan
    You do Say scan daily if you want it automatic

    This asks Claude to run the scan again on its own each day, so you don't have to remember to.

    You get reports/ a fresh report every morning
  7. 7
    Scan
    You do Click any job row in the console
    You get job detail three sections: why this job, behind the score, the posting
  8. 8
    Scan
    You do Click Queue on jobs you like
    You get queue your shortlist
  9. 9
    Apply
    You do /apply <company> — read the plan it shows you, reply go

    Claude shows you what it's about to build before it builds anything — typing "go" is your green light to proceed.

    You get applications/ a full application package: tailored résumé, honesty check, gap analysis
  10. 10
    Apply
    You do Or /apply <link you found online>

    Paste any job posting URL after the command to build a package for a job you found outside the console.

    You get applications/ same package for any job post URL (it won't show in the console — that's normal)
  11. 11
    Apply
    You do Read gap-analysis.md before applying
    You get gap-analysis.md an honest read on where you match and where you don't
  12. 12
    Apply
    You do Edited your résumé by hand? Ask for a re-lint

    Just ask Claude in plain words, e.g. "re-lint this" — it re-runs the honesty check on your edited version.

    You get honesty-check.md your edit gets checked too
  13. 13
    Interview
    You do /interview-prep <company or job link> + your round

    Tell Claude the company (or paste the job link) and which interview round you're prepping for — e.g. "first round" or "onsite."

    You get interview-prep/ company research, interviewer dossier, and a flip-card practice deck
  14. 14
    Interview
    You do Later rounds: tell it the round number and how the last one went
    You get interview-prep/ a new deck, earlier ones kept

When things go differently — totally normal

None of these are errors. Click any one to see what's happening and what to do next.

Claude says it can't scan — no Indeed connector

What you'll seeClaude tells you plainly that it can't run the scan yet — it will never invent jobs just to give you an answer.

What to doAdd the Indeed connector to your Claude account, then run /scan-jobs again.

The scan found nothing

What you'll seeThe report says so honestly — an empty report, not a fake one.

What to doWiden your titles or location in a Claude session, or just scan again tomorrow.

You never replied "go" to the apply plan

What you'll seeNothing was created — on purpose. Claude waits for your green light before building anything.

What to doNothing to clean up. Reply go whenever you're ready.

The job link won't open (LinkedIn etc.)

What you'll seeClaude explains it can't open the page and asks you to paste the posting text instead.

What to doCopy the job description from your browser and paste it into the chat.

A job you applied to via link isn't in the console

What you'll seeThat's expected — the console only shows scan reports, not every application you've built.

What to doNothing — your package is safe in applications/.

Your computer/Claude crashed mid-build

What you'll seeThe folder is marked INCOMPLETE, so it's obvious nothing got lost silently.

What to doRun /apply <company> again — it resumes where it left off.

The console page shows "Can't load data"

What you'll seeThis happens when you opened the file directly instead of through a server.

What to doRun the serve command it shows you, then use the localhost link (see Step 4 above).

"process my queue" didn't build anything

What you'll seeClaude can't see the console's Queue button — it will show you a list of queued jobs instead.

What to doName the jobs you want it to build applications for.

The honesty check flagged something (NO SOURCE)

What you'll seeThe system caught a claim your résumé can't back up.

What to doThis is the feature working, not a bug — fix the line or add the fact to your registry. Never send a flagged résumé.

protecting you, not blocking you
Claude asked which of two similar jobs/folders you meant

What you'll seeA lettered list of options — Claude never guesses when things are ambiguous.

What to doPick from the list; the recommended one is marked for you.
